Quantum Cloud Storage Product Brief

Quantum Cloud Storage offers encrypted object storage for small and medium businesses.
The product supports unlimited versioning, automatic backups every 6 hours, and
99.95 percent uptime guaranteed under the Silver service tier.

Pricing starts at 0.02 dollars per gigabyte per month. The Gold tier adds 24/7 phone
support and a dedicated account manager. Data is stored in three geographic regions
by default, and customers may choose a single region to meet residency requirements.

Supported integrations include Slack, Jira, and GitHub.
